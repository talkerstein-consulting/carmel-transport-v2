# Hero shot — text-to-video prompt

## Prompt

Photorealistic aerial drone footage, single continuous take, bright midday sun in a dry
desert landscape. A blue semi truck hauls a 40-foot shipping container with a pale
sky-blue corrugated roof and dark teal-blue sides along a wide four-lane divided highway.

The drone flies directly behind the truck and moves at exactly the truck's speed, so the
truck stays locked in the centre of frame the entire time. The shot opens in a low chase
position just above and behind the trailer, looking forward down the highway with the road
receding to the horizon. Over the course of the shot the drone climbs smoothly and tilts
down in one slow, continuous, perfectly even motion, ending in a clean flat top-down view
looking straight down at the container roof. The tilt is gradual and constant from the
first frame to the last, with no pauses, no speed changes, no jolts and no re-framing.

Painted flat on the container roof is a large circular company decal, oriented upright so
it reads correctly from the overhead camera, with its top edge toward the truck cab. The
badge is a round emblem with a blue globe and faint latitude-longitude grid, an orange
city skyline across the upper half, and a stack of multicoloured shipping containers with
a yellow reach stacker along the lower half. Across the middle in bold condensed serif
capitals is the word CARMEL in black with a white outline, and directly beneath it the
word TRANSPORT in smaller white capitals. The decal is flat paint that follows the roof's
corrugated ribs and the roof's perspective, lit by the same sunlight as the rest of the
roof, with no glow, no embossing, no reflection and no drop shadow. It stays locked to the
roof for the entire shot, moving and rotating with the truck, never sliding, drifting,
flickering, warping or changing size.

The highway has two lanes in each direction, a solid yellow line along the inner edge,
white dashed lane markings and a white outer edge line, on pale grey sun-bleached asphalt.
The truck passes through a four-way intersection where a crossing road meets the highway,
with metal guardrails curving around the corners and dry tan scrub and gravel on the
shoulders. Hard midday sunlight comes from the right, casting a short dark shadow of the
truck onto the road to its left.

Photorealistic, sharp, natural colour, shot on a professional drone camera. No cuts, no
transitions, no text overlays, no watermark, no other vehicles, no people.

cuts, scene change, different truck, different container colour, watermark, subtitles,
people, other vehicles, cartoon, illustration, painting, low quality, artifacts

## Settings

kling3_0 --mode pro --duration 5 --sound off --aspect-ratio 16:9   (7.5 credits)
No start_image, no end_image — text only, so the camera move is generated as one
continuous motion instead of being interpolated between two pinned frames.
