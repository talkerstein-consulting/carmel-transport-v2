Direction A

Stencil & Steel

The container door becomes the design system. Stats sit in a real placard block — the bordered, hairline-ruled data panel stencilled on every container — instead of in cards. Hazard chevrons, hard edges, zero radius, outlined display type. Motion is mechanical: things slide and lock, with a hard stop, like a twistlock engaging.

Direction B

Bay Plan

The ship's stowage grid runs the whole layout. Every section sits on a visible container grid, and the fleet is drawn literally — 300 cells, filling in sequence like a load plan being committed. Reads like an operations screen rather than a brochure, which is the actual promise: these people track your box.

Direction C

Night Yard

Cinematic and quiet. Sodium light on wet steel, deep vignetting, and type that finally stops shouting — light-weight Roboto Slab at large size with one word carrying the emphasis. Yellow is the only light source in the palette; it glows rather than fills. The most premium of the three, and the least like a trucking website.
